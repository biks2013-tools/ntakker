ntakker
=======

Install: drag "ntakker" onto "Applications", then open it from Applications.
It appears as an icon in the menu bar (there is no Dock icon).

First recording: macOS asks for Microphone and Screen Recording. Allow both;
Screen Recording is how macOS lets an app capture system audio (no video is
recorded). If you choose Apple Cloud transcription, macOS also asks for Speech
Recognition.

Transcription providers and their keys, and the Ask (Q&A) provider, are set in
ntakker's Settings; they are saved in ~/.tool-agents/ntakker/.env.

Companion apps from the same release page:
  ntakker studio  - study finished recordings (transcripts, cuts, minutes)
  ntakker-cli     - the same recorder from the terminal

Requires macOS 26 or later.

ntakker studio
==============

Install: drag "ntakker-studio" onto "Applications", then open it from Applications.

What it is: the companion app to ntakker. It opens the recordings ntakker saved
(audio + transcripts), plays them with the transcript following along, lets you
jump to any line, cut parts out into a new copy, rename speakers, fix transcript
text, re-transcribe, search every transcript, and ask questions or write meeting
minutes with your AI provider.

First launch: if ntakker is set up on this Mac, the studio uses ntakker's recording
folder. Otherwise it asks you to choose the folder that holds your recordings.

Ask, minutes and re-transcription use ntakker's settings
(~/.tool-agents/ntakker/.env): NTAKKER_ASK_PROVIDER and the provider's key.

Requires macOS 26 or later.
